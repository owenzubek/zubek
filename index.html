About

ZUBEK

ZUBEK is a commercial video production company based in Cleveland, Ohio, specializing in branded content, music videos, and documentary work. The studio holds FAA Part 107 certification, folding licensed aerial coverage into every production without a second vendor. Photography is available on request for clients who want stills alongside their video coverage.

We work in small, senior crews rather than large agency teams — the same people who write the treatment are behind the camera on shoot day and in the room for the edit. That keeps budgets lean, decisions fast, and every deliverable consistent with the pitch that sold it.

Founded and directed by Owen Zubek, a Polish-Bohemian filmmaker whose work includes projects for Machine Gun Kelly, Denzel Ward, Lululemon, and independent documentary subjects across Northeast Ohio. Owen leads every production personally, from first call to final export.
