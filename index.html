Biography

ZUBEK is a Cleveland-based commercial video production company specializing in branded content, music videos, and documentary work, with photography available on request. The studio holds FAA Part 107 certification, and aerial coverage is brought in whenever a production calls for it. Founded and directed by Owen Zubek, whose credits include Trippie Redd, Brooks Running, and Güdtaste, the crew stays small end to end — the person who plans the shoot is the same one behind the camera and in the edit.
